# 🛒 UrbanMart: Retail Sales & Inventory Optimization [![MySQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/MySQL-8.0-blue?logo=mysql&logoColor=white)](https://www.mysql.com/) [![Power BI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Power_BI-Desktop-F2C811?logo=powerbi&logoColor=black)](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/) [![DAX](https://img.shields.io/badge/DAX-Calculations-orange)](#dax-kpis) [![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

An enterprise retail analytics and supply chain intelligence project designed to track customer lifetime value (CLV), stock turnover velocity, and inventory health across **600 active SKUs** and regional retail fulfillment centers using MySQL and Microsoft Power BI.

---

## 📌 Executive Overview & Challenge

UrbanMart managed multiple product lines across regional distribution centers. High inventory carrying costs and delayed visibility into slow-moving categories were compressing net operating margins:
- **Stagnant Working Capital**: Management lacked real-time visibility into SKUs with excessive weeks-of-supply (>18 weeks), tying up capital in stagnant inventory.
- **Reporting Inefficiencies**: Departmental sales reports were compiled manually in Excel each week, consuming hours of manual labor per reporting cycle.
- **Customer Retention Gaps**: Marketing teams lacked visibility into customer repeat purchase cadence and customer lifetime value (CLV) tiers.
